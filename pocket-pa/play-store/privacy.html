Pocket PA — Privacy Policy

Effective November 6, 2026

Pocket PA is an independent, offline Android microphone and voice-effects app.

Microphone

When you press Start, Pocket PA captures live microphone audio, processes it on-device and plays it through your chosen media output. It does not save recordings, upload audio, or transmit audio to servers. The microphone stops when you stop it or Android ends the service.

Bluetooth and notifications

Bluetooth permission, if granted, is used to identify and display the name of connected audio devices. Notifications show when the microphone is live and include a Stop control.

Collection and retention

The app has no internet permission, advertising, analytics, or user account. It does not collect, share, sell, or store personal data. Selected settings (such as voice effects, gain and noise-gate preferences) are stored on your device and removed when you clear app data or uninstall it.

Your controls

You may revoke permissions in Android Settings, turn off the mic using the app or notification, or uninstall at any time. Keep your phone away from loud speakers to prevent feedback.

Contact

For support or privacy questions, open an issue on GitHub.
