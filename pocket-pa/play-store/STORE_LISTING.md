# Instant Mic — Google Play listing draft

**App title:** Instant Mic: Phone to Speaker

**Short description:** Turn your phone into a Bluetooth mic with 18 fun voice effects.

## Full description

Your pocket-sized microphone is ready whenever you are.

Instant Mic turns your Android phone into a live microphone for your Bluetooth speaker or other selected audio output. Make announcements, practice karaoke, speak to a group, or experiment with real-time voice effects — without creating an account and without an internet connection.

Features:
- Start and stop live microphone audio with one tap
- Use your connected Bluetooth speaker or another media output
- 18 live voice effects: Clean, Man, Woman, Child, Monster, Ghost, Deep, Helium, Robot, Alien, Chipmunk, Whisper, Echo, Cave, Radio, Telephone, Megaphone and Distortion
- Optional device-dependent acoustic echo cancellation
- Mic input gain, speaker output gain, and noise-gate controls
- Live microphone level meter
- Foreground notification with a Stop control
- Offline operation; no ads, accounts or audio uploads

Bluetooth audio can introduce noticeable latency depending on the speaker and Android device. Keep the phone away from loudspeakers to reduce feedback. Voice pitch effects are designed for fun, not professional voice processing.
