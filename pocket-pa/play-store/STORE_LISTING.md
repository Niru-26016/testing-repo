## Full description

Use your Android phone as a live microphone for a compatible Bluetooth speaker, car media system, earbuds or headphones. Pair your accessory in Android Settings, choose an audio output in Instant Mic, and tap Start.

- Send live microphone audio through a compatible media output.
- Start and stop from the app. With notifications allowed, you can also stop from the live microphone notification.
- Check the input meter and adjust mic gain, output level and the noise gate, which starts on for fresh installs.
- Choose Clean, Men, Robot or Helium from the voice panel. Tap Show more options for 14 more presets.
- Reduce low-frequency rumble with the built-in filter. Adjust Android noise suppression and echo reduction separately on supported phones.
- Use Instant Mic offline, with no account or ads. The app does not save audio recordings.

Bluetooth playback delay and compatibility vary by phone and accessory. Echo reduction cannot eliminate Bluetooth delay or feedback. Start with low output volume and keep the phone away from the speaker.
