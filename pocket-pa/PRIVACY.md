# Privacy Policy — Pocket PA

**Effective date: November 6, 2026**

Pocket PA is an offline microphone and voice-effects app for Android, developed as an independent project. This policy explains its use of device data.

## Audio and microphone

The app asks for microphone access when you tap **Start Microphone**. Sound is processed in memory on the phone, with optional live voice effects, then sent to the device's chosen audio output (such as a connected Bluetooth speaker). **Pocket PA does not record or store audio**, does not upload sound, and does not transmit microphone data to any server. Mic capture stops when you press Stop, stop the foreground notification, or the Android system stops the service.

## Bluetooth and notifications

On supported Android versions, Bluetooth permission is requested only to detect or display connected audio output device information. Notifications are used to indicate active microphone streaming and provide a Stop action. You can deny notifications without disabling the core mic function.

## Data collection, sharing, retention

Pocket PA has no internet permission, no advertising SDK, no analytics SDK, and no tracking. It does not collect, share, sell, or retain personal data. Settings such as selected voice preset, levels, and gate sensitivity are saved locally using Android SharedPreferences. You can delete these settings by clearing app storage or uninstalling the app.

## Children and safety

The app does not knowingly collect information from children. Loud output can cause feedback or hearing discomfort; start with a low speaker volume and keep the phone away from the speaker.

## Controls

Microphone, Bluetooth and notification permissions can be changed any time in Android Settings. The app is designed to function completely offline.
